My project is looking how external pressures affect different mental health disorders. The idea is to hopefully create a tool that allows people to predict what mental health issues they are likely to have based on their external pressures. Specifically I found a dataset which contains a student mental health survey where students rate how much pressure for academics, financial pressure, and social pressure they feel. It also contains how strong they feel different mental health disorders, ie depression, anxiety, and isolation. I would like to use this data to create a tool that predicts which mental health issues a student is likely to have based on their external pressures. There are a lot of other parts of the dataset I would like to look at like which majors experience more mental health issues etc.
