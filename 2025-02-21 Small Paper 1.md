## Theory: Connectionism
Connectionism is a cognitive science framework where cognition is formed from the interconnectedness of simple, small processing units. This theory was inspired by the structure of the brain, that is interconnected neurons to form a network of processing units. Connectionism is commonly used in AI through neural networks, where there are parallel smaller processing units that are connected together to come to the final conclusion. Philosophers have taken an interest in connectionism as it offers a different approach to cognition than the Theory of Mind, which is based on the idea that cognition is a single entity and similar to a computer.
