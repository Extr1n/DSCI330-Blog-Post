# Post 150 words on your blog about material anchors for mental computation.

Another example of a material anchor for computation is your fingers! We as humans will use our 10 digits quite extensively. Growing up, we use our fingers to count various things which helps anchor the numbers to something concrete. Typically people also use them for multiples of a number. For example, 5 10 15 20 .... Using your fingers this way is a very common thing to quickly find multiples of a number. Additionally, as a CS major I sometimes end up counting binary on my hand. In this case, each finger represents a bit and having a finger up represents a one in that bit whereas finger down means a 0. Ultimately, there are so many different ways of using your fingers to aid mental computation. Arguably, your fingers are one of the most versatile material anchors for aiding mental computation. However, some people look down on people using their fingers for counting or other computation related things. In my opinion, looking down on someone using their fingers to count is like looking down on someone who uses a bike or scooter instead of walking. Both fingers and a bike are tools that can be used to assist you in your endeavors, so why not use your fingers?
