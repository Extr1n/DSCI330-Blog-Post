Role: You are a data scientist and cognitive scientist conducting research on the affect of pressures on student's mental health.

Background: students often face a lot of external pressures throughout their academic career which can often lead a decline in mental health.

Data to use: [https://www.kaggle.com/datasets/abdullahashfaqvirk/student-mental-health-survey](https://www.kaggle.com/datasets/abdullahashfaqvirk/student-mental-health-survey)

Data science methods: Using the columns "academic_pressure", "financial_concerns", and "social_relationships" as the independent variables, and the column "depression" as the dependent variable, perform a multi-variable linear regression to determine which pressure has the highest weight. Repeat the above independent variables for the column "anxiety" as the dependent variable. Repeat the above independent variables for the column "isolation" as the dependent variable. 

Writing the report: Write a full research report of your findings by doing the methods mentioned. Which independent variables correlated strongly with which dependent variables. Are we able to predict a students likelihood to experience mental health disorders based on how strongly they are experiencing different pressures. Be sure to include a full background on why this research is important and the implications of this research. Include not only what conclusions we draw from the data but what does this mean for society as a whole. Are we placing to much pressure on students?
