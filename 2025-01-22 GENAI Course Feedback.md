# Take the short course at Getting Started with Generative AI in Education for Students. Take the survey at Student Reviewer Survey. Post 100 words of feedback on your blog. How could this short course be improved?

One thing I was skeptical about going into this course was that it would be mostly common sense. However, it did cover important ways to optimize AI's output such as the RTRI method. I enjoyed the RTRI part (its typically how I prompt an AI) but I think a key part of gen AI that was not thoroughly covered is refining output of AI. For example, one time I used gen AI to create practice problems to study but it would give problems that either were irrelevant to the course content or were not solvable (the AI's proposed solution was incorrect). As such, learning to refine AI output is also a key part of using AI that was not covered thoroughly in this course.

Additionally, I appreciated how the course was setup and designed to be interactive and very visually appealing. This was achieved through a wide variety of drop down menus, try it yourself sections and even short videos. Overall I found the course to be a nice baseline for people who are not super experienced in the world of generative AI and was quite informative. However, as mentioned above I wish it went more in depth on fine tuning results / clarifying things to the AI.
