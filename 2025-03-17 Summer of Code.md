[Invesalius](https://summerofcode.withgoogle.com/programs/2025/organizations/invesalius)

Invesalius is a tool used to render 3D medical imaging by using CT scans or MRI scans. This allows professionals to thoroughly analyze scanned brains in 3 dimensions as opposed to the standard flat imaging of CT or MRI scans. This is interesting to me because it adds a whole new level of depth to analyze and explore. Being able to generate 3D interactive images of complex human anatomy and brain structures is simply fascinating to me. This is important for studying higher-order cognitive processes because it would be a way for researchers to analyze in real time how the brain handles cognitive tasks. For example, having subjects reason through problems of varying difficulty and analyzing which parts of the brain are more activated in real time would lead to a further understanding of how the human brain functions under various cognitive loads.
